# S7.0 — 4K read test (27–28 Sep 2026, laptop; decisions F74, F75)

`scripts/analyze_footage.py` on four candidate clips (the only four with ≥ 3 reads in the 25 Sep survey) and two zero-read controls, at `--max-width` 1920, 2560 and 3840, with `--frames 30 --ocr-per-frame 8`. The same 30 frame indices are sampled at every width (they depend only on the clip length), so each clip is an A/B on identical pixels. The real stack: YOLOX-S on DirectML, then PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 mobile on the 8 largest vehicle crops a frame. Raw records: `read-test-w1920.json`, `read-test-w2560.json`, `read-test-w3840.json`.

**Runs:** w1920 23:39–00:25 IST, w2560 00:25–01:07, w3840 01:07–01:51. All three ran **while the live platform ran** (worker on the sandbox cameras, 28 local publishers, lane B's session). The laptop was paging (0.4–0.8 GB free, 900–9,500 pages/s), and the last ~30 min of w3840 overlapped the S7.1 render of 13270133. **Timings (detect ms, OCR ms / crop) are therefore load-confounded** and are not a clean cost-versus-width curve; the read counts are unaffected.

**How to read "distinct full plates":** registrations after `plates.coerce`, which is how the pipeline stores them. The count still includes OCR variants of one plate (e.g. `MH02DJ0770` / `MH02DJ8770` / `MH02OJ0770` in 13269027), so it is an upper bound on real vehicles, not a precision figure. S7.1's spot check gives the precision.

- **Gate: passed.** 3840 px yields ≥ 5 distinct full plates on four clips, so S7.1 renders the S7.0 clips, not the two-clip fallback.
- **Render clips for S7.1**, by distinct full plates at their best width: `13270133` (43), `13269027` (27), `13172888` (15), `13105330` (8). The first two are the "best two" of the Phase 7 cut order (item 5). All four are rendered with OCR crops cut from the **full 3840 px frame** (F74).
- **The controls stay at zero at every width.** 4K does not create reads where no plate is legible, and it does not rescue those two clips.
- **The smallest vehicle behind any full read** was 246 source px wide (p10 ≈ 330, `13270133`), which sets S7.1's `--min-vehicle-w 240`.
- **`own01` stays 1080p (F75, offline half).** On `13270133`, 2560 px gave 56 full reads against 47 at 1920 px, which is 1.19×, below F75's 1.5× bar. No 1440p candidate was encoded, and S7.4 uses `D:\projects\sentinel-footage\own01.mp4` (1080p, 30 s black pre-roll, made 27 Sep 12:35).
- **Watch plates for the render and the dry run** (clip time; add 30 s for `own01`'s pre-roll):
  1. **`MH02GB4920`**: frames 30–611 (1.0–20.4 s), a stationary car in the jam. Reads: 10 at 1920, 7 at 2560, 9 at 3840, mean conf 0.90.   3. **`MH04HY7362`**: frames 785–843 (26.2–28.1 s). Reads: 1 / 2 / 2, conf up to 1.00, the same text at every width.
  - v2.6's default `MH02EX1995` (~41 s) was **not read at any width** in these 30 frames; only the misread `MH02EK1995` appeared at 2560/3840 (frame 1250). Do not use it as the watch plate.
